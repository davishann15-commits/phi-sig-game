# Original house doors

These meshes come from the doors already modeled inside the imported house chunks. Each `SM_OriginalLeaf_*` mesh is an original moving room leaf; each `SM_OriginalRoom_*` mesh is the corresponding chunk with moving geometry removed. The four ground-floor porch entrances use eight `SM_OriginalPorchFrame_*` and `SM_OriginalPorchGlass_*` assets extracted from the original framed glass doors. Their fixed jambs, brick, and glass transoms remain in `SM_OriginalRoom_Entrance_p0_*` assets.

`Content/HouseReview/original_house_doors.json` records room hinge positions, closed angles, and which leaves face areas without a playable route. `Content/HouseReview/original_porch_doors.json` records the porch panels and pairs. `Content/Python/build_existing_house_doors.py` applies both plans to the campaign map. The imported room visuals have collision disabled because the house already has separate `COL_*` collision chunks; moving leaves provide their own collision until clear of the doorway. The porch leaves use moving player collision proxies. Five imported room leaves lacked a usable handle, so the builder attaches `SM_HouseLeverHandle` to those leaves. Each porch leaf also gets that two-sided handle near the meeting stile. The other imported room handles remain as modeled.

The campaign map uses `SMF_Entrance_p0_m1_BenchClear` and `COLF_Entrance_p0_m1_BenchClear` for the first porch furniture chunk. These variants move only the existing bench 310 cm into the wall bay between door pairs. The visual actor has no collision; the matching `COLF` actor supplies its original furniture collision with the bench box moved to match. Their FBX sources are kept in this folder for reimport. The generic door importer skips them so a full door rebuild does not reset their collision settings.

For a full rebuild, `Content/Python/export_original_house_meshes.py` exports the source chunks and glass assets. `extract_original_house_doors.py` splits the room leaves in Blender; copy its generated FBX files from `/tmp/sso_extracted_house` into this folder. `extract_original_porch_doors.py` then splits the porch frames and panes using the exported originals and the preserved `Baselines/SM_OriginalRoom_Entrance_p0_p0_Solid.fbx`. `create_door_handle.py` rebuilds the handle asset. Run `Content/Python/import_original_house_doors.py` before rebuilding the campaign map. The FBX files stay here so Unreal can reimport them without depending on temporary files.

## Basement service alcove door pose

`SM_OriginalLeaf_Basement_m1_p1_Solid_01` is already in its modeled closed position at yaw `0`, along the alcove's east wall. The leaf's source-world bounds are X 1584.4–1592.9 cm, Y -542.7 to -391.5 cm, Z -329.1 to -123.1 cm. Its white jambs and lintel are in `SM_Basement_m1_p1_Solid`: the jambs sit at Y -551 to -543 and -391 to -383 cm, and the lintel runs over the leaf at Z about -123 cm. The correct `doorwayCenter` for interaction and inspection is `(1588.7, -467.1, -211)` cm. The hinge remains `(1588.7, -391.5, -321)` cm. `closedYaw=-90` rotates the leaf out of this frame into the open alcove, so it must stay `0` in the map and `original_house_doors.json`.

The concrete floor patch in `SM_Basement_m1_p0_Solid` covers X 1242.0–1594.8 cm and Y -607.5 to -265.5 cm (352.8 by 342.0 cm). A distinct west-wall opening at X about 1242 cm, Y -522 to -351 cm provides the alcove entrance. The framed east-wall leaf does not lead through the wall: connected collision component #7 of `COL_Basement_m1_p1_Solid` is a continuous wall at X 1594.8–1612.8 cm, Y -616.5 to -256.5 cm. Keep this door locked until a real modeled destination and matching wall opening exist. `COL_Basement_m1_p1_DoorClear` removes only source leaf proxy component #8; it preserves wall #7 and does not make the doorway traversable.

## South striped room door collision

`SM_OriginalLeaf_Upper_m1_m2_Solid_03` belongs to the separate `SM_Upper_m1_m2_StripedDoorframe_Solid` at UE X about -2335 cm, Y about -355 cm. Its hinge remains `(-2361.4, -266.9, 310)` cm. In the framed closed pose (`closedYaw=180`), the leaf runs from Y about -443 to -267 cm; `doorwayCenter=(-2361.4, -355.0, 420)` cm. `openYaw=90` gives a 90-degree swing into the west room. The floor on both sides of this frame is about Z 301 cm.

The source `COL_Upper_m1_m2_Solid` contains an old, unmoving collision proxy for this leaf in its yaw-0 source pose at X -2372.51 to -2350.33 cm, Y -266.94 to -90.62 cm. `create_southstriped_door_clear.py` removes only this 12-face connected component from the preserved `Baselines/COL_Upper_m1_m2_Solid.fbx` and writes `COL_Upper_m1_m2_DoorClear.fbx`. The generator verifies 448 retained faces after FBX round-trip with maximum vertex shift below 0.002 cm. Import the variant as a distinct mesh with complex-as-simple collision, then assign it only to the `COL_Upper_m1_m2_Solid` campaign actor. The south wall, floor, and every other collider component stay in place.
