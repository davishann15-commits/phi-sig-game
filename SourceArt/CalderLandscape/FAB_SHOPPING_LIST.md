# Fab shopping list: Calder in the mountains

Updated 2026-10-15. Every link below was checked on Fab at **$0 for both the Personal and the Professional licence, with no end date** (Saved/CalderWork/plan/fab_approval_list.md has the full notes). If a page shows a price, skip it: several Megascans share a name with a paid twin.

**How to add:** use Fab's **Add to project** and pick SeniorSendoff, the same way as the six assets now in `Content/Environment/Megascans`. Use quality **High**. The 2K cap is applied when the game is packaged, so a High download doesn't make the game bigger. Megascans singles are about 60–80 MB each on disk.

## Already in the project (don't re-add)
- **Animal Variety Pack:** the 17 materials were re-linked to the pack's own textures on 2026-10-15, so the pig, deer, fox, wolf and crow are textured again.
- **Megascans Trees:** European Beech and Norway Maple.
- **Megaplant Norway Spruce** (`/Game/Megaplant_Library`): the ProceduralVegetationEditor and DynamicWind plugins are now on in the .uproject, so its materials and wind load. Rendering on Mac (Nanite foliage and assemblies) is still untested.
- **In `/Game/Environment/Megascans`:**
  - Beech Fern, Black Locust, Raspberry, Large Fallen Tree, Rotten Tree Stump and Nordic Forest Cliff Large, all added by Fab.
  - **Lady Fern** and **Mossy Forest Boulder**, imported by Claude from your downloads in Fab's own layout and settings (`Tools/claude/import_fab_extras.py`).

## Skip
- Electric Dreams: a huge sample project; not needed.
- Low Poly Cow: low-poly, doesn't match the realism bar.
- The free sound packs (Essential Ambience SFX, Animal Sound Effect Pack): free only under the Personal licence, which caps revenue. Skip them if the game might ever be sold.
- The Animal Variety Pack's **wolf** (wolves were gone from Appalachia by 1986) and its **stag** (a European red deer, not a whitetail).
