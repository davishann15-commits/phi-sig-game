# Pole integration and actual-map checks

Native source is `HouseClubPole.h/.cpp` and `HouseClubPoleSmoke.cpp`. The owned-pawn RPC and existing HUD/input hooks are in StoryCampaign.h/.cpp. All route points are actor-local floor/feet positions. The code applies the actor transform exactly once; it does not read HouseWorldScale.

Planned corrected room location: actor origin current-world `(-1935,-1175,.88)` with scale 1. LowerShaft `(0,45,0)`, UpperShaft `(0,45,331.65)`, LowerLanding `(0,150,0)`, UpperLanding `(0,150,331.65)`. These are editor-editable. No absolute room coordinates are baked into native behavior or tests.

PoleBase `(0,0,0)` and PoleTop `(0,0,545)`. The default mesh is the engine cylinder, fitted in BeginPlay when bAutoFitPoleMesh is true. Set its material to the chrome material. If assigning a custom artist mesh, set bAutoFitPoleMesh false and fit component transform in the scene.

SafetyGateHinge (GatePivot) local `(-65,110,386.65)`; GateVisual defaults child center `(65,0,0)`, a cube top rail. When replacing with custom gate art, fit GateVisual's relative transform against that hinge (do not assume custom art has a centered origin). SafetyGateCollision local `(0,110,386.65)`, box extents `(65,8,55)` permanently blocks all normal players. The reserved user's capsule ignores ONLY this component; world shell, floors, rail, pole, props and all other collision remain enabled. The visual gate opens 100 degrees while occupied and closes after completion/abort. Guard gap must face +Y.

Native standalone check (actual E input and full swept-capsule traversal):
`UnrealEditor SeniorSendoff.uproject /Game/Story/Maps/Chapter01_House -game -HouseClubPoleSmoke -UserDir=/tmp/club-pole-solo -ResX=960 -ResY=540 -windowed -log -ABSLOG=<evidence>/pole-solo.log`

Explicit success marker: `CLUB_POLE_SMOKE_PASS`. It checks supporting perimeter floor on 12 sides of the aperture, physically walks into the closed gate, uses real E down/up, samples continuous motion, asserts precise landing and restores the normal capsule size/input/collision. Failure marker: `CLUB_POLE_SMOKE_FAIL`, or any `CLUB_POLE_ABORT`.

Natural interaction is checked separately from exact aiming: the player starts 110 cm to the side of the top landing, checks normal standing eye height at yaw offsets +/-28 degrees and pitch +/-28 degrees, and walks to the closed gate. After sliding, the player actually walks around the solid lower shaft to its rear and uses E from there with an offset standing aim. `CLUB_POLE_NATURAL_FOCUS_PASS` and `CLUB_POLE_NATURAL_WALK_PASS` record these cases; facing away must not show a prompt. Ordinary interaction covers a 210-cm visible-shaft approach (minimum 180 cm for old saved radius overrides). The top zone stays outside the closed gate. Server-authored entry alignment routes wider upper approaches through the gate opening and lower rear approaches around the solid shaft; every segment is collision checked and swept.

Actual 2-process LAN check (normal lobby join, readiness and seamless story travel):
1. Start listen server: `UnrealEditor SeniorSendoff.uproject /Game/Story/Maps/Lobby?listen -game -ClubPoleMultiplayerSmoke -UserDir=/tmp/club-pole-host -port=7781 -ResX=640 -ResY=360 -windowed -log -ABSLOG=<evidence>/pole-host.log`
2. Start a separate client: `UnrealEditor SeniorSendoff.uproject 127.0.0.1:7781 -game -ClubPoleMultiplayerSmoke -UserDir=/tmp/club-pole-client -ResX=640 -ResY=360 -windowed -log -ABSLOG=<evidence>/pole-client.log`

The verified lobby asset is `Content/Story/Maps/Lobby.umap` (`/Game/Story/Maps/Lobby`). The opt-in test waits for two real connected lobby members, selects distinct characters, uses the normal ready/start-story calls and follows their normal travel into Chapter01_House. Production story late-join policy is unchanged. Do not start this LAN test with Chapter01_House?listen.

Both logs must explicitly contain `CLUB_POLE_MP_PASS`; exit0 alone is not sufficient. After setup, the remote client sends an opt-in owned-pawn ready RPC only after 64 steady frames and five seconds without a loading gap. The host waits for this real acknowledgement and its own steady frames before actual E slide. The remote owning client then walks to the bottom and performs actual E climb; both processes must observe at least four actual movement samples in each direction. A client competes for the occupied pole; no second journey can begin. Completion counters, endpoints, plausible movement steps, restored walking and local input are checked. Final client arrival gets a bounded two-second reconciliation window, with exact current/expected positions logged. Replicated ambience readiness gets 60 seconds to allow cold loading; all required playback/phase/volume assertions still run. The overall test is bounded at 200 seconds and needs its own UserDir; no shipping automation is active.

Client ride motion uses a monotonic receipt-anchored elapsed clock. The first server clock estimate can seed only a plausible age of -180 to +150 ms, so later GameState clock corrections cannot reverse or skip a ride. The replicated journey retains a successful-versus-aborted end flag. Successful client completion follows the remaining collision-tested route with a short smooth finish before releasing input and the specific gate exception. Abort restores real gravity without forcing an endpoint. All route motion remains swept against world geometry.

Diagnostics: `CLUB_POLE_START`, `CLUB_POLE_COMPLETE`, `CLUB_POLE_REPLICA_BEGIN/END` (net mode, owned-local flag, travel serial, estimated/anchored age, sample count, largest position step), `CLUB_POLE_RECONCILE_BEGIN/COMPLETE/BLOCKED`, `CLUB_POLE_MP_REMOTE_READY_SENT/RECEIVED`, `CLUB_POLE_MP_FINAL_LANDING_BEGIN/PASS`, `CLUB_AMBIENCE_MP_READINESS`, `CLUB_POLE_REJECT`, `CLUB_POLE_ABORT`. Occupant, CompletedJourneys, AbortedJourneys and bLastCompletedUp are replicated and inspectable.
