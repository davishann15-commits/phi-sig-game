# Smooth woodland path and more even ground

Only the Unreal woodland was changed.

- The trail follows one continuously sampled curve; shared ribbon edges and smooth normals remove the old angular strip joins.
- Dirt blends into the surrounding grass over the path margins instead of ending at a hard line.
- Woodland ground uses the photographed lawn textures and palette from the house, with much smaller coverage variation and fewer bare patches. The house lawn material is unchanged.
- The woodland border blends back to the original yard coverage.
- A centered approach keeps the final curve clear of the shack doorway.

The two new saved assets are `M_WoodsGround` and `M_WoodsTrail` in the existing ExteriorNature content folder. They are generated by `install_materials.py`. The native exterior builder assigns their textures at load.

Build logs, native camera captures, actual player route checks, and saved-map hash checks are in `evidence/`. No new real lights or foliage batches were added.
