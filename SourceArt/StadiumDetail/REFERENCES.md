## Candidate visual gates

At1–2m: discern plank seams, rolled edges, fastening points, bracket shadows/material separation, concrete aggregate/cracks and realistic scale. At eye level: aisle steps must look usable, not like63cm climbing ledges. At50yards: press windows/decks/platform should form readable layers, and scoreboard hierarchy should resolve24–17/00:00 first, secondary statistics second. Avoid a stadium covered in uniformly noisy rust, glowing signage, giant bolts or perfectly identical litter. Period references guide proportions/material use; no claim of photographic equivalence before candidate views are inspected.
