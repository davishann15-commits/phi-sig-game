# Rear woodland rocks and undergrowth

Updated only the Unreal project. The saved Chapter01_House map is unchanged; the exterior scenery is generated by the existing native exterior builder.

- Rounded irregular rocks with mineral grain, hairline cracks, surface normal relief, moss coloring, and three palettes. Four larger stone clusters are off the trail.
- 382 shrubs and 16,007 taller grass clumps, with green/dry variation and existing wind materials.
- Nine shared foliage batches; no decorative foliage collision, dynamic shadows, or indirect-light updates. Grass fades between 28.8 and 40 meters; shrubs between 54 and 75 meters.
- Stones have simple blocking collision; planting stays out of the trail, hut clearing, and stone footprints.

## Evidence

Build, native scene, camera captures, full route walk and saved-map reopening evidence are in `evidence/`. The frame-rate comparison uses the same 1280x720 quality-2 cameras as the prior woods version. Ground-level woods/hut captures measured 38.9–47.2 FPS; the prior captures measured 42.2–50.2 FPS. These are local development measurements with ordinary run-to-run variation. The final polish only removes plants that overlap rocks.

The scenery checks are single-player; no multiplayer session was run for this visual update. Existing tree interactions are unchanged.
